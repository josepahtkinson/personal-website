For our family and friends joining us from afar...

Flights

Augusta Regional Airport (AGS)

The most convenient option. AGS is a quick 15-minute drive from downtown Augusta and our reception venue.

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL)

Often offers more direct flights and better rates. ATL is about a 2-hour and 15-minute drive to the Augusta area.

Accommodations

The Augusta Marriott

We have secured a block of rooms at a discounted rate. This hotel is located downtown, just minutes from the Sacred Heart Cultural Center.

Book with our Group Rate

The Partridge Inn

A historic, boutique option located in the beautiful Summerville neighborhood of Augusta. We do not have a formal block here, but highly recommend it!

Visit Website

Getting Around

Rental Cars & Rideshares

While Uber and Lyft are readily available in the downtown Augusta area near the reception, we recommend renting a car if you are flying into Atlanta or wish to easily travel between Grovetown and Augusta.

A few of our favorite local spots to check out while you're in town...

Eats & Coffee

Mirin Fusion Kitchen

Our go-to spot everytime we go back to Evans! They have incredible Asian fusion dishes and a really great, modern atmosphere.

Takosushi (Evans)

A fun and eclectic mix of Mexican, Southwestern, and Asian food (including amazing sushi). Highly recommend checking out the Evans location.

Rooted Coffeehouse

The perfect spot to grab your morning latte. We love their coffee and it's a great little relaxing environment to start your day.

Explore Augusta

Augusta Riverwalk

A beautiful brick walking path situated directly along the Savannah River. It's a classic Augusta experience and a great way to enjoy the weather.

Savannah River Brewing Co.

If you're looking for a relaxed place to grab a drink and hang out, this local brewery is a fantastic spot right in downtown Augusta.
