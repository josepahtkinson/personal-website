A few details to help you prepare for our big day...

Please kindly respond by December 1st, 2026, so we can finalize our numbers with our caterer and venues.

Black Tie Optional. We would love to see our family and friends get dressed up for our big day! For the ladies, think midi to floor-length gowns. For the men, please wear tuxedos or dark-colored suits with a tie. Mass will be held in a Catholic Church and will call for a great degree of reverence. Please be respectful in dress during the mass, including the length and fit of dresses.

Due to the large number of guests, we ask that you please arrive by 12:30 pm to allow time for parking and finding seats. The processional for the mass will begin promptly at 1:00 pm.

We love your little ones! Children under 4 years of age are more than welcome to join us, though please note they will not be assigned their own formal seating in order to maximize space. Any children over the age of 4 must be explicitly listed on your RSVP to attend.

The Nuptial Mass at St. Teresa of Avila and the main reception at Sacred Heart will both be held indoors. However, weather permitting, we will be hosting a lovely cocktail hour in the beautiful outdoor garden area at Sacred Heart before moving inside for the evening!

Yes! Both the church in Grovetown and the Cultural Center in Augusta have ample, free parking available on-site for all of our guests.

We are incredibly blessed to have a large number of loved ones wanting to celebrate with us! Due to strict venue capacity limits, we are only able to accommodate guests who are specifically named on your invitation. Augusta Regional Airport (AGS)

Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta (ATL)

Getting Around

Rental Cars & Rideshares

While Uber and Lyft are readily available in the downtown Augusta area near the reception, we recommend renting a car if you are flying into Atlanta or wish to easily travel between Grovetown and Augusta.

A few of our favorite local spots to check out while you're in town...

Eats & Coffee

Mirin Fusion Kitchen

Our go-to spot everytime we go back to Evans! They have incredible Asian fusion dishes and a really great, modern atmosphere.

Takosushi (Evans)

A fun and eclectic mix of Mexican, Southwestern, and Asian food (including amazing sushi). Highly recommend checking out the Evans location.

Rooted Coffeehouse

The perfect spot to grab your morning latte. We love their coffee and it's a great little relaxing environment to start your day.

Explore Augusta

Augusta Riverwalk

A beautiful brick walking path situated directly along the Savannah River. It's a classic Augusta experience and a great way to enjoy the weather.

Savannah River Brewing Co.

If you're looking for a relaxed place to grab a drink and hang out, this local brewery is a fantastic spot right in downtown Augusta.
